How it works

From registration to keys

Register →
  1. 01

    Registration

    Tell us what you need: type of home, region, budget and start date. It takes a minute and costs nothing.

  2. 02

    Mandate

    We put the agreement in writing. Only once you have signed the mandate do we start searching.

  3. 03

    Search

    We search for homes that match your profile and only put forward options that fit.

  4. 04

    Match and signing

    We arrange the viewing, check the landlord and review the lease with you. You sign the lease yourself, and only then is the fee due.

Fee

One fee. Only when you sign.

1.5× your total monthly rent, including gas, water and electricity, plus 21% VAT. Due only when a lease is signed through us.

  • No costs upfront and no registration fee. If you don’t sign a lease through us, you pay nothing.
  • Homes you found yourself, or were already offered, are excluded from the fee.
  • Only the tenant pays. Landlords never pay us anything.
  • You have a 14-day withdrawal period. We only start within that period if you explicitly ask us to.

FAQ

What to know before you start

Who do you work for?

Only for the tenant. We do not work for landlords and receive nothing from them.

Why are there no homes on this site?

We do not publish listings. We search per tenant, based on your search profile. That keeps us fully on your side.

What if you find nothing?

Then you pay nothing. The fee is only due when you sign a lease through us.

What do you do with my data?

We only use your details to contact you and to search for a home for you. We share them with a landlord only when you ask us to.

Privacy Policy

Can I stop?

Yes. You can end the mandate before you sign a lease, and no fee is due then.
