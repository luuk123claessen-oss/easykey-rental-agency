The Shield

We check the landlord and the lease

Rental scams are real. Before you sign or pay anything, we look at who is renting out the home and what the lease says. Point at a line to see what we check.

Rental agreement · sample

Sample only. No real person or property.

01

Landlord and owner

We check that the person offering the home is the owner, or is authorised by the owner. Ownership is recorded in the public land registry (Kadaster).

Source
02

Payment details

The name on the bank account should match the landlord, or someone authorised to collect the rent. Anything else is raised with you before any money moves.

Source
03

Rent against the legal limit

We compare the basic rent with the rules for its segment. Limits from 1 January 2026: social rent up to € 932.93; middle rent between € 932.93 and € 1,228.07 (or 144–186 points); above that is the free sector (187+ points). In the free sector the Huurcommissie can check the starting rent, within the first 6 months.

Source
04

Deposit

For leases signed from 1 July 2023 the deposit may be at most 2 months’ basic rent. It must be returned within 14 days after the lease ends, or within 30 days if costs are deducted.

Source
05

Unusual or one-sided clauses

We read the contract for clauses that look unusual or one-sided and explain them in plain language. Agreements and the deposit must be confirmed in writing (Wet goed verhuurderschap, since 1 July 2023).

Source
06

No money before keys and contract

No payment before there is a signed contract. Key money is not allowed, and neither are costs for drawing up the lease. Be wary of anyone who asks for money just to view a home.

Source

What we check. General information, not legal advice.
