FAQ

What to know before you start

Who do you work for?

Only for the tenant. We do not work for landlords and receive nothing from them. That is why you are allowed to pay us for the search.

Why are there no homes on this site?

We do not publish listings. We search per tenant in our network, based on your search profile. That keeps us fully on your side.

What if you find nothing?

Then you pay nothing. The fee is only due when you sign a lease through us.

What happens with my data?

We only share your details with a landlord when you want us to.

Can I stop the assignment?

Yes. You can cancel before you sign a lease. No fee is due then.
